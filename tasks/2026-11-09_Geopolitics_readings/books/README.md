# Books for Geopolitics readings

The assignment requires reading *The Art of War* and one more book from the list, then writing a reflection on paper.

**Suggestion for the second book:** *The Prince* is the quickest to read and pairs well with Sun Tzu, since both are classics about power. For a modern link to US–China relations, choose *Destined for War*.

**Important:** these reviews were written from general knowledge of the books, not from reading the full texts. Dates and names are accurate to the best of my knowledge, but check details before using them, and add your own impressions for the reflection.
