# HRM project: prep notes for the case presentation (Yelyzaveta's case)

The group notes (`materials/hrm_presentation_notes.txt`) do not say which Kahoot question or case belongs to Yelyzaveta. These notes cover all four questions and the bonus question, so they can be used for whichever case is assigned. Each case lasts 4 minutes: explain the facts, leave time for the class to vote, then explain the answer with course content.

## Suggested 4-minute structure (any case)
1. **Situation (60 s):** state the facts from the Kahoot question in your own words, and say what HR decision is needed.
2. **Vote (60 s):** show the Kahoot question and give the class time to answer.
3. **Answer (90 s):** reveal the result, say why this choice fits the situation, and link it to a concept from the course.
4. **Transition (30 s):** one sentence to the next case.

## Evidence that is verified
- **Bonus question (which method best predicts job performance):** structured interview. Schmidt and Hunter (1998, *Psychological Bulletin*) reported a validity of about 0.51 for structured interviews versus 0.38 for unstructured. Sackett et al. (2022, *Journal of Applied Psychology*, 107(11), 2040–2068) revised the estimates downwards to about 0.42 versus 0.19, and structured interviews still rank at the top. All figures were found in secondary sources, so check the original papers before quoting them on a slide.
- **Not verified:** the often-quoted onboarding statistics (for example "82% higher retention" or "30% of hires leave in 90 days"). The searches only found vendor blogs that repeat each other, so do not put these numbers on a slide unless you check SHRM or Talya Bauer's research.

## The four questions: reasoning to adapt (use the course material for concepts)

**Q1 Selection: 12 agents in 3 weeks, 300 applications.** Which style fits? *Amélie* (same grid and work-sample test for everyone) gives fair, comparable decisions at scale. *Giovanni* is faster and friendlier but decides by instinct, so it is less consistent. A defensible answer is "both, in two rounds": a structured screening first, then a short human conversation for fit. Point to the bonus question: structured methods predict performance better.

**Q2 Onboarding: 30% of new hires leave within 6 months.** *Amélie* offers a 90-day plan with clear objectives and check-ins (clarity, compliance). *Giovanni* offers a buddy system and culture rituals (connection). The two fix different gaps (role clarity versus belonging), so the strongest answer is both. Prepare one sentence explaining why each gap causes early exits.

**Q3 Performance: a top salesperson who hurts the team.** The review must cover results *and* behaviour. *Amélie's* formal appraisal does that; *Giovanni's* informal coaching and celebration of results would reward the harmful behaviour. Link this to performance appraisal criteria.

**Q4 Diversity: 60% of hires come from referrals and the team looks the same.** The problem is the sourcing channel. *Giovanni's* proactive sourcing in new communities widens the pool; *Amélie's* anonymised CVs and standard criteria help fairness but only after candidates have applied. Prepare a sentence on why referral-heavy hiring reproduces a similar team.
