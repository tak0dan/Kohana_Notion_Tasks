Video 1: Amélie (≈ 2 min, ~260 words)

[0:00–0:20] Introduction “Hello, I’m Amélie. I’m 34, and I’m an HR Business Partner at a large industrial group. I’ve spent eight years working with managers on hiring, performance and workforce planning.”

[0:20–0:55] Background “I started as an HR assistant, then moved into recruitment, and I now support three business units. I’m certified in HR analytics, and I built our group’s competency framework. Every role has clear criteria, and every decision is documented.”

[0:55–1:30] Strengths “What I do best is create fairness through structure. Same questions for every candidate, clear objectives for every employee, indicators to track what works. I’m comfortable with compliance, labour law and reporting. When something goes wrong, I like to find the root cause in the data.”

[1:30–1:50] Weaknesses “I know I can be slow. I like to check before acting, and I’m less comfortable when a situation is ambiguous or changes every week. Sometimes people tell me I’m a bit formal.”

[1:50–2:00] Closing “I believe good HR is fair, consistent and evidence-based. Thank you for your time.”



Video 2: Giovanni (≈ 2 min, ~260 words)

[0:00–0:20] Introduction “Hey, I’m Giovanni, 29. I’m a talent recruiter at a scale-up that grew from 40 to 300 people in three years. I love meeting people and finding where they’ll shine.”

[0:20–0:55] Background “I started in a startup, where I was doing everything: sourcing, interviews, even office parties. I now lead recruitment, and I built our employer brand from nothing. Most of our best hires came from my network and from events I organised.”

[0:55–1:30] Strengths “I’m good at reading people and making candidates feel welcome. I move fast, and I know how to sell a company without lying. I also care about culture, so I always make sure the newcomer has someone to talk to on day one.”

[1:30–1:50] Weaknesses “My weak spot is process. I decide a lot on instinct, and I don’t always document why. I know that can create bias, and it’s something I’m working on.”

[1:50–2:00] Closing “For me, hiring is first a human conversation. Let’s talk!”
